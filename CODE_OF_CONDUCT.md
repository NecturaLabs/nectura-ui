# Code of conduct

Canopy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any
other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to support@necturalabs.com, with "Conduct" in the subject.
Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block people
who break this code.
